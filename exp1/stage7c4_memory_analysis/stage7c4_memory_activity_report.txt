13. Main finding
Supported explanation: A, as an observed association. GLB total action counts decrease from 1,648,828 at 16 PE to 944,472 at 64 PE; GLB read actions decrease from 512,352 to 267,912, GLB update actions decrease from 492,480 to 253,440, and GLB idle actions decrease from 643,996 to 423,120. The GLB Accelergy output decreases by 2,638,276 output units over the same comparison. The data supports that the GLB energy reduction accompanies lower GLB action counts, but it does not establish a constant energy-per-action relationship or causality beyond the model outputs.
MAC and PE SPAD action counts increase from 16 to 64 PE, while DRAM total action counts decrease. This explains why the total change is smaller than the GLB decrease alone.
