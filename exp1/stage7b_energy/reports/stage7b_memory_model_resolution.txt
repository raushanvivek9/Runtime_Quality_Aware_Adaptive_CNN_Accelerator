4-7. Parameter meanings
-----------------------
SRAM_row_size=2: documented SRAM row/block transfer granularity.
SRAM_bank_size=5: documented temporal capacity for SRAM rows per bank; unused by
the current extractor.
DRAM_row_size=2: documented DRAM row/block transfer granularity and used in DRAM
idle-action calculations.
DRAM_bank_size=5: documented temporal DRAM capacity per bank; unused by the
current extractor.
