=================================

Exact values:

The same source file defines:

- Dataflow: ws
- InterfaceBandwidth: CALC

Documented meaning (README_accelergy.md):
- SRAM_row_size is the size of the row buffer/block loaded by each SRAM access.
- SRAM_bank_size is the temporal capacity for retaining previously accessed data,
  with more than one row buffer per bank.
- DRAM_row_size and DRAM_bank_size have the analogous DRAM meaning.

Actual consumption in this checkout:
- preprocess.py reads SRAM_row_size and DRAM_row_size and embeds them in the
  generated create_action_count.sh command.
- create_action_count.py uses SRAM_row_size only indirectly through the generated
  command? In the inspected implementation, SRAM_row_size is parsed but the SRAM
  idle formulas use ArrayHeight/ArrayWidth, not SRAM_row_size.
- create_action_count.py uses DRAM_row_size directly in the DRAM idle formulas.
- SRAM_bank_size and DRAM_bank_size are not consumed by preprocess.py or
  create_action_count.py in this checkout.
- The generated Accelergy architecture.yaml contains SRAM depth/width/bank
  attributes and DRAM width/type attributes, but not these four parameter names.

Therefore these values are action-count extraction metadata in this integration,
not direct Accelergy energy coefficients. They affect the generated action counts
and thus the action quantities Accelergy consumes, especially DRAM idle actions.
They do not alter the SCALE-Sim performance simulation itself.
