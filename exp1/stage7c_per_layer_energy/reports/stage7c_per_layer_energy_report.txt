STAGE 7C-1 PER-LAYER ENERGY REPORT
===================================

1. Objective
------------
Characterize conv1, conv2, and conv3 independently at 16 PE / 4x4,
32 PE / 4x8, and 64 PE / 8x8. Each workload was run independently through
SCALE-Sim, official action-count extraction, and Accelergy.

2. Source and environment
-------------------------
Isolated source:
/home/cs25m115/Neural_Acc/exp1/stage7c_per_layer_energy/source/SCALE-Sim-v3-energy

This source is copied from the Stage 7B-passing source and retains the validated
scalar fix. All runs used -i conv and the validated 64 KiB, one-bank,
bandwidth-10, weight-stationary energy model with DRAM_row_size=2 and
DRAM_bank_size=5.

The protected SCALE-Sim checkout, Stage 5/6 inputs, Stage 7A evidence, and
Stage 7B results were not modified.

3. Layer topology
-----------------
The copied one-row topologies preserve the exact source values:
- conv1: 32x32 input, 3 channels, 3x3 filter, 16 filters, stride 1
- conv2: 16x16 input, 16 channels, 3x3 filter, 32 filters, stride 1
- conv3: 8x8 input, 32 channels, 3x3 filter, 64 filters, stride 1

10. Component energy breakdown
------------------------------
The actual component names support these direct aggregates:
MAC, GLB_SRAM, DRAM, and PE_scratchpads. Their sums equal each YAML Total.

11. Reconstruction check against Stage 7B
------------------------------------------
Isolated sums exceed Stage 7B complete-workload totals by 24.0, 40.0, and
48.0 output units for 16, 32, and 64 PE respectively. These small differences
are retained and explained as independent-run boundary/memory-state effects;
no forced matching was performed. The isolated cycle sums match the Stage 7B
cycle totals exactly: 137797, 71833, and 39773.

12. Scientific limitations
--------------------------
- Accelergy values are estimated/simulated, not physical hardware measurements.
- Energy units are reported exactly as provided by the YAML; no unit was inferred.
- No pJ/MAC conversion was introduced.
- No arbitrary power model was introduced.
- No energy was inferred from cycle count.
- The topology is convolution-only.
- Classifier energy is not included.
- Results correspond to fixed 16/32/64 PE configurations.
- The adaptive controller has not been evaluated.
- Stage 7C-2 was not started.
