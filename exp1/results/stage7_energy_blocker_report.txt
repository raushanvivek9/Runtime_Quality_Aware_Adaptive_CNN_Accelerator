3. Why this blocks Stage 7
--------------------------
Stage 7 requires a defensible estimated/simulated energy model for the PE-array configurations:
- 16 PE
- 32 PE
- 64 PE

The project has validated cycle data for those configurations, but no valid energy methodology was available to map the same workload, dataflow, bandwidth, and memory assumptions into energy. Without a validated energy model, any energy number would be an arbitrary assumption rather than a measured or simulator-supported estimate.

This project must not use:
- arbitrary joules/MAC
- arbitrary pJ/MAC
- arbitrary PE power
- arbitrary SRAM energy
- arbitrary leakage power
- unqualified “energy” derived from cycle count alone

Those would be fabricated and non-defensible experimental results.
