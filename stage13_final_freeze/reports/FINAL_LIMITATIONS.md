# Final Limitations

- Analytical cycles are not measured hardware latency.
- Full ResNet18/VGG16 RTL correlation was not performed.
- Stage 8-11 RTL results are reduced-workload evidence.
- No physical FPGA/ASIC timing, power, or energy claim is made.
- Accelergy units were not converted into joules.
- Sparse execution uses exact-zero activation operands.
- Adaptive PE allocation is analytical.
- Test data is not used for policy selection.
- Results are limited to the evaluated models, datasets, checkpoints, and methodology.
